## What Aurora does

Aurora previews supported Windows gaming-related settings, creates backups for
supported changes, applies selected user-level optimizations, verifies current
values, and provides undo where supported. It also provides RAM/process monitoring
and NVIDIA/AMD guidance.

Results depend on the PC, Windows version, hardware, drivers, games, and existing
settings. Aurora does not guarantee a particular FPS increase, latency reduction,
or other performance improvement.
