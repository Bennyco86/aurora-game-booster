# Aurora Game Booster Refund Policy

Updated: 2026-07-29
Status: Draft for owner/legal review before paid sales

## Microsoft Store purchases

Aurora's initial paid release is planned through Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles
the customer transaction and determines refund eligibility under the applicable
Microsoft Store Terms of Sale, the product offer, and applicable law.

Microsoft states that digital goods such as PC apps are not automatically refundable
unless the offer or applicable law makes the purchase eligible. This policy therefore
does not promise an automatic 14-day change-of-mind refund.

To request a refund, use the Microsoft account/order support process linked below.
If the online option is unavailable in your market, contact Microsoft Sales support.

## Product support

For installation or product problems, email `nzbennycohen@gmail.com` with the Aurora
version, Windows version, and a short description. Do not send passwords, payment-
card details, complete registry backups, or unrelated logs.

Product support cannot approve or process a Microsoft Store payment refund, but it
can help document a product issue for a refund request.

## Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies that apply under law, including
applicable rights under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act. If Microsoft or
Aurora's product offer provides greater rights, those greater rights apply.
